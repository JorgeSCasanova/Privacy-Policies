IMPOSTER — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how IMPOSTER ("the App"), developed by JorgeSCasanova, handles information when you use the application.

IMPOSTER is a party game that does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your real name, phone number, or email address.

1. Information Stored Locally

IMPOSTER stores certain information locally on your device using the application's internal storage. This information may include:

  • Player names entered for games.
  • Game settings and preferences.
  • Language preferences.
  • Categories created or configured by the user.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to or stored on servers operated by the developer.

2. Advertising

The free version of IMPOSTER displays advertisements provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), a service provided by Google.

Google may collect and process certain information from your device and interactions with advertisements in order to provide, measure, and improve advertising services. This may include information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and information related to ad interactions, subject to Google's own policies and the settings available on your device.

Premium users do not receive advertisements within the App.

For more information about how Google handles data, please see Google's privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Premium Purchases

IMPOSTER offers a one-time Premium purchase through Google Play Billing.

Payments and purchase processing are handled by Google Play. The App does not collect or store your payment card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

The App receives information from the Google Play billing system necessary to determine whether the Premium purchase has been completed or restored.

4. Third-Party Services

IMPOSTER uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — for displaying advertisements in the free version of the App.
  • Google Play Billing — for processing the one-time Premium purchase and restoring purchases.

These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Retention

Information stored locally by IMPOSTER remains on your device until you delete it through the App, clear the application's data, uninstall the App, or otherwise remove the stored information using your device's settings.

The developer does not maintain a server containing the locally stored player names, game settings, or categories.

6. Children's Privacy

IMPOSTER does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The App does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information to the developer.

Because the App uses third-party advertising services, advertising and data practices may also be subject to Google's policies and the applicable settings and requirements for the user's age and region.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to limit the information handled by the App. Information such as player names and game settings is stored locally on the device rather than on servers operated by the developer.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding personal data processed by third-party services, including rights of access, deletion, restriction, or objection where applicable.

Since the developer does not operate a server storing the locally saved player names or game settings, those locally stored data can generally be removed by deleting the relevant application data or uninstalling the App.

For information concerning data processed by Google services, please refer to Google's privacy tools and privacy policy.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable laws, or the services used by the App.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
